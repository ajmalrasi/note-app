DESIGN EXPLORATION

Meet Daynote.

A familiar place to capture things, with a timeline that remembers when you wrote them.

  • Switch Day, Week, and Month to regroup the same notes.
  • Try search, labels, the calendar, and grid or list layout.
  • Open a card to edit it. Changes stay in this browser.
  • Pin, archive, trash, and restore the sample notes.
This is a design prototype with sample content and a fixed demo date of 22 October 2026. It is not connected to your Raspberry Pi. Browser storage can be cleared.
